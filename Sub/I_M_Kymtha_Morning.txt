Ninte aanandavum santhoshavum kondu enne thrupthi aakkenanme,/ ksheenam ulla ente asthikal santhoshikkum.| Ente papangalil ninnu ninte thiru mukham thirichu / ente athikramangal okkeyum mayikkaname.

h3:Psalms 63
Ente dhaivame nee ente dhaivam aakunnu; njaan ninakku aayi kaathu irikkum.

Dhaahichu varandu vellathinnai aagrahikunna bhoomi pole ente aathmavu ninne kurichu dhahichu irikkunnu. Ente jadavum ninakkai kathu irikkunnu.

1-2 Aakaashangal dhaivathinte mahathwathe ariyikkunnu. / Aakaashathattu avante kaivelaye kaanikkunnu. / Pakal pakalinu vakkine ucharikunnu; / raathri raathrikku arivine ariyikkukayum cheyyunnu.

3-4 Avayude shabdham kelkkapedatha bhashayum illa vaakkukalum illa. / Bboomiyil ellaa idavum avayude ariyippum, / bhoolokathinte athirthikalolam avayude vachanangalum purapettu irikkunnu.

5 Avan avayil sooryante mel avante koodaaram adichu; / athu thante manavarayil ninnu purappedunna manavaalan enna pole akunnu. / Paraakramam ullavan enna pole tbante vazhi oduvan santhoshikkum.

6. Aakaashathinte attangalil ninnu athinte purappaadum, / aakaashathinte aruthikalinmel athinte thaangalum aakunu. / Athinte aaviyil ninnu maravu aayirikkunnathu onnum illa.

h3:St. Luke 1: 46-55
Mariam paranjathu enthennaal, ente dhehi karthaavine pukazhthunnu. Ente aathmavu enne jeevippikkunnavan aaya dhaivathil santhoshichu. Enthennaal avan thante dhasiyude thaazhchaye nokkikkandu irikkunnu. Kandaalum ithu muthal sakala vamshangalum enikku bhagyam tharum.

Avan thante bhujam kondu jayam undakki; thangalude hrudhaya vicharathil ahankaarikal aayavare chinnichu; avan balavaanmaare simhaasanangalil ninnu marichu idukayum, thaazhma ullavare uyarthukayum cheythu.

h3:Psalm 113 (1-9)
Prakaashathinte srushhtaavinu sthuthi; karthaavinte bhruthiyanmaare sthuthi paaduvin. Ningal karthaavinte naamathe sthuthippin. ​

Karthaavinte naamam aadhi muthal ennekkum vazhthapettathu aayirikkatte.

Sooryante udhayam muthal athinte asthamanam vareyum karthaavinte naamam valiyathu aakunnu.

Karthaavu sakala jathikalkum mel unnathanum thante mahathwam aakashangalkku meetheyum aakunnu. 

Uyarathhil vasikkukayum aazhathil nokkukayum, cheyyunna nammude dhaivam aaya karthaavinodu sadrushan aakashathilum bhoomiyilum aarullu.

Avan eliyavane janathinte prabhukanmaarodu koode iruthendathinu kuppayil ninnum uyarthunnu. Avan machi aayavale makkalude santhosham ulla mathavu aayi bhavanathil vasikkumar aakkukayum cheyyunnu.
h3:Psalm 148​​​
Aakaashathil ninnu karthaavine sthuthippin. Unnathangalil avane sthuthippin.

Avante sakala dhoothanmarume avane sthuthippin. Avante sakala sainyangalume avene sthuthippin.

Adhithya chandhranmare avane sthuthippin : praakasham ulla sakala nakshatrangalume avane sthuthippin., Aakashangalude aakashangalum aakashangalkku meethe ulla vellangalume avane sthuthippin. Ava karthaavinte naamathe sthuthikkatte.​

Enthennaal avan paranju ava undayi. Avan kalpichu ava srushhtikkapettu. Avan avaye ennekum aayi sthirapeduthi. Niyamam nischayichu. Athu azhiyunnathum alla.

Bhoomiyil ninnu mahaa sarppangalum, ella aazhangalum, agniyum, kalmazhayum, uracha vellavum, himavum avante vachana pravarthi aaya kaahtukalum, kodunkahtukalume karthaavine sthuthippin.​

Parvathangalum, sakala kunnukalum; phala vrukshangalum, sakala kaarakilukalum, mrugangalum sakala kannukaalikalum; izha janthukkalum pakshikalum, paravakalume karthaavine sthuthippin.

Bhoomiyile rajakkanmarum, sakala janangalum, bhoomiyile prabhukkanmarum, sakala nyayadhipanmarum, shishukkalum, kanyakamarum, vrudhhanmarum, youvanakkarum, karthaavinte naamathe sthuthikate.

Enthennaal avante naamam maathram valiyathu aakunnu. Avante mahathwam bhoomiyilum akashathilum undu. Avan thante janathinte konpum, thante sakala neethimaanmarkum thaniku adutha janam aaya Israel makkalkum mahathwavum uyarthunnu. 
h3:Psalm 149​
Karthaavinu puthiya paattum, neethimaanmarude sabhayil avante sthuthiyum paaduvin. Israel thante srushhtaavil  santhoshikkukayum, sehiyonte puthranmaar thangalude rajavil anandhikkukayum cheyyatte.

Avar thappukalaalum, chathura thappukalaalum avante naamathe sthuthikkukayum, veenakalal avanu paadukayum cheyyatte. Enthennaal karthaavu thante janathil ishtapedunnu. Avan dharidhranmarkku reksha kodukkukayum cheyunnu.

Neethimaanmaar bahumaanathil balapedum, avar thangalude kattilukalin mel avane sthuthikkukayum thangalude kantangal kondu dhaivathe pukazhthukayum cheyyum.

Purajaathikalkku prathikkriyayum janathinu shaasanayum koduppanum, avarude rajakkanmare aalaathukal kondum avarude manyanmaare irumbu changalakal kondum bandhippanum, ezhuthappettu irikkunna nyaya vidhhi avarude mel nadathuvanum, avante sakala neethimaanmaarkum mahatwam undakkuvaanum avarude kaikalil irumuna vaalundu.​
h3:Psalm 150 ​
Karthaavine avante shudhha sthalathu sthuthippin. Avante shakthiyude aakaashathattil avane sthuthippin.

Avante vallabhathwathil avene sthuthippin. Avante shreshhtathayude bahuthwathil avane sthuthippin. Kombinte shabdathal avane sthuthippin. Thamburukalaalum veenakalaalum avane sthuthippin.

Thappukalaalum chathurathappukaalaum avane sthuthippin. Maadhhuryam ulla kambikalaal avane sthuthippin. Naadham ulla kaithaalangalaal avane sthuthippin. Shabdathaalum aarppuviliyaalum avane sthuthippin. Ellaa shwaasavum karthhaavine sthuthikkatte.

Sakala jaathikalume karthaavine sthuthippin, sakala janangalume avane sthuthippin, ethennaal avante krupa nammude mel balapettu irikunnu. Avan sathyam aayittum ennekum karthaavu aakunnu.

Dhaivame sthuthi ninakku yogyam aakunnu. Barekmor. †Shubaho...  Men olam...
