h3:Another Eniyono
Kanyaka aaya dhaiva maathaave! ella kaalathum ella nerathum ninte praarthana njangalkku kotta aayirkkename.

(Karthaave!) ella naazhikayilum njangalkkayi kenikal vaykkunna dushhtane, ninte parishudhhanmarude praarthanayal njangalil ninnu mayichu kalayename.

Njangalude karthavu aaya yeshu mashiha! ninte sleeba njangalkku kotta aayirikkukayum, athinu akathu njangal maraykkappedukayum cheyyename.

Vachanam aaya yeshu thampurane! jeevanode irikkunnavare ninte sleebayal kathukollukayum, marichupoyavare ninte karunayaal punyappeduthukayum cheyyename.

Naam jeevanode irikkunna naal okkeyum, ellayippozhum pithavineyum, puthraneyum, vishudhha roohayeyum sthuthichu vandhichu mahathwappeduthanam.

Ehtavum karuna ullavan aaya njangalude dhaivame! nee ezhunnellunna ninte aa valiya dhivasathil ninte valathhu bhagathhu njangale niruthename.

Ee samayathhu ninakku sthuthi paaduvan njangale nee yogyar aakkiyirikkakondu ninte raajyam anubhavippanum njangale yogyar aakkename.

Njangalude maranathinteyum jeevanteyum naadhan aaya karthaave! ninte krupa njangalude mel undayi njangaleyum njangalude marichu poyavarudeyum aathmakkalin mel karuna cheyyename. Karthaave! krupa cheythu sahayikkename.

h3:Psalm 148​​​
~i~Urangiyavare! ningal unarnnu ezhunnehtu sthuthippin.~i~ Aakaashathil ninnu karthaavine sthuthippin. Unnathangalil avane sthuthippin.

Avante sakala dhoothanmarume avane sthuthippin. Avante sakala sainyangalume avene sthuthippin.

Adhithya chandhranmare avane sthuthippin : praakasham ulla sakala nakshatrangalume avane sthuthippin., Aakashangalude aakashangalum aakashangalkku meethe ulla vellangalume avane sthuthippin. Ava karthaavinte naamathe sthuthikkatte.​

Enthennaal avan paranju ava undayi. Avan kalpichu ava srushhtikkapettu. Avan avaye ennekum aayi sthirapeduthi. Niyamam nischayichu. Athu azhiyunnathum alla.

Bhoomiyil ninnu mahaa sarppangalum, ella aazhangalum, agniyum, kalmazhayum, uracha vellavum, himavum avante vachana pravarthi aaya kaahtukalum, kodunkahtukalume karthaavine sthuthippin.​

Parvathangalum, sakala kunnukalum; phala vrukshangalum, sakala kaarakilukalum, mrugangalum sakala kannukaalikalum; izha janthukkalum pakshikalum, paravakalume karthaavine sthuthippin.

Bhoomiyile rajakkanmarum, sakala janangalum, bhoomiyile prabhukkanmarum, sakala nyayadhipanmarum, shishukkalum, kanyakamarum, vrudhhanmarum, youvanakkarum, karthaavinte naamathe sthuthikate.

Enthennaal avante naamam maathram valiyathu aakunnu. Avante mahathwam bhoomiyilum akashathilum undu. Avan thante janathinte konpum, thante sakala neethimaanmarkum thaniku adutha janam aaya Israel makkalkum mahathwavum uyarthunnu. 
h3:Psalm 149​
Karthaavinu puthiya paattum, neethimaanmarude sabhayil avante sthuthiyum paaduvin. Israel thante srushhtaavil  santhoshikkukayum, sehiyonte puthranmaar thangalude rajavil anandhikkukayum cheyyatte.

Avar thappukalaalum, chathura thappukalaalum avante naamathe sthuthikkukayum, veenakalal avanu paadukayum cheyyatte. Enthennaal karthaavu thante janathil ishtapedunnu. Avan dharidhranmarkku reksha kodukkukayum cheyunnu.

Neethimaanmaar bahumaanathil balapedum, avar thangalude kattilukalin mel avane sthuthikkukayum thangalude kantangal kondu dhaivathe pukazhthukayum cheyyum.

Purajathikalkku prathikkriyayum janathinu shaasanayum koduppanum, avarude rajakkanmare aalaathukal kondum avarude manyanmaare irumbu changalakal kondum bandhippanum, ezhuthappettu irikkunna nyaya vidhhi avarude mel nadathuvanum, avante sakala neethimaanmaarkum mahatwam undakkuvaanum avarude kaikalil irumuna vaalundu.​
h3:Psalm 150 ​

h3:MAALAAKHAMAARUDE STHUTHI
Athyunnathangalil swargeeya maalaakamaar sthuthikkunnathu pole, balaheenarum manmayarum aaya njangalum sthuthichu parayunnu.

Kaalam okkeyilum, neram okkayilum, uyarangalil dhaivathinu sthuthiyum, bhoomiyil samaadhaanavum nirappum, manushya makkalkku nalla sahranavum, (undayirikkatte)

Njangal ninne sthuthikkukayum, vaazhthukayum, vandhikkukayum cheyyunnu. Sthuthiyude shabdam ninakku njangal karettunnu.

Sarva shakthi ulla pithaavum, swargeeya rajavum srushhtaavum aakunna dhaivam aaya karthavum, yeshu mashiha aakunna eka puthran aaya dhaivam aaya karthaavum, parishudhha ruhaayum aakunna ninne ninte sthuthiyude valippam nimitham njangal sthothram cheyyunnu.

Pithavinte puthranum, vachanavum, lokathhinte paapathe vahikkunnavanum vahichavanum aaya dhaivathinte kunjadu aaya karthaave! njangalodu karuna cheyyename.

Lokathhinte paapathe vahikkunnavanum, vahichavanum aayullove! ninte chevi chaayichu njangalude apeksha kaikkollename.

Pithaavinte valathu bhaagathu mahathwathode irikkunnavane! dhaya thonni njangalodu karuna cheyyename.

Enthennaal nee mathram parishudhan aakunnu. Pithaavaam dhaivathinte mahathwathinu, vishudhha roohayodu koode yeshu mashiha! nee mathram karthavu aakunnu. 

Ella kaalathum njangal jeevanode irikkunna divasangal okkeyum ninne njangal vaazhthukayum, ennekkum vazhthappettathum, nithyatha ullathum aaya ninte parishudhha thiru naamathe sthuthikkukayum cheyyum. 

Njangalude pithaakkanmarude dhaivam aaya sarvashakakthi ulla karthaave! nee vaazhthappettavan aakunnu. Ninte thirunaamam sthuthikkappettathum ennekkum sthuthikalal prabalappettathum aakunnu. 

Sthuthi ninakku yogyam aakunnu. Mahathwam ninakku yuktham aakunnu. Sakalathinteyum dhaivavum, sathyathinte pithavum aayavane! ninakkum, eka puthranum, jeevan ulla vishudhha roohaykkum, ippozhum, ella samayathum ennekkum pukazhcha chercha aakunnu. Amen. 
h3:Concluding Prayer
Moron eshu mishiha! ninte karunayude vaathil njangalude mukhangalil nee adaykaruthe. Karthaave! njangal paapikal aakunnu ennu njangal ettu parayunnu. Njangalodu karuna undakename.

Karthaave! ninte maranathaal njangalude maranam mayappeduvan aayittu ninte sneham ninte sthaanathu ninnu njangalude pakkal ninne irakki. Njangalodu karuna undakename.
