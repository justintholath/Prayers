h3:Aprem's Bovooso
Njangalkk ulla karthaave!, - Njaan iha nidhra ozhinjittu, - Unarvode nin thiru munpil, - Nilpaan enikku nee nalkaname. 
Veendum njaan urangunnaakil, - Enikk ulla ente urakkam, - Karthaave! nin thiru munpil, - Dosham koodaathe aakkaname.
Enn unarchhayil njaan chathi pedukil, - Nin nanmayil njaan porukkappedum, - Urakkathil njaan pizhachhenkil, - Poruppaan karuna nee cheyyaname.
Thava ksheenathin skeeppaayaal, - Nalla urakkam enikku nee thaa, - Aakaa swapnam ashudhhiyil ninn - Enne nee rekshichhu kollaname.
Nirappu niranja urakkathil, - Raav okkayum enne nee bharikka, - Thannyavarum vendaa ninavum, - Ennil mushkaram aakkalle.
Ninte adiyaan njaan athinaal - Ente sandhukkal kaappaan aay, - Velivinude Maalaakaaye, - Enikku nee tharanam Karthaave.
Dveshatha petta apekshayil ninn - Enne nee rekshichhu kollaname, - Uyirpetta nin dehathe, - Njaan anubhavichh ennu athinaale.
Njaan charinj anukoolathaal urangumbol, - Ninte chora enikku kaaval, - Nin manassinude swatha karmam, - Nin krupayode nalkaname.
Nin kay mananja shareerathil, - Ninte valathheth aakaname, - Ninte karunakal kotta athaay - Enikku nee chuttichhu kollaname.
Shareeram adangi urangumbol, - Kaaval athaay athu nin shakti, - Sourabhyam aaya dhoopam pol - Ente urakkam thiru munpil.
Ninne pett enna ammayude, - Ninnod ulla apekshayaale, - Enikk ulla shayanathinmel, - Thinmappettavan anayaruthe.
Enikku vendeett undaay enna - Ninakk ulla poojayaale, - Enne vyasanathil aakkaayvaan, - Saathaane nee mudakkaname.
Karthaave njan paranj appo - Ente pakkal thikakkaname, - Ninakk ulla sleebaayaal, - Ente aayussu kaakkaname.
Njaan unarappettenn appol, - Ninne njaan kond aaduvaan, - Ente thalarchhayude pakkal, - Ninte upavi nee kaattaname.
Nin thiru manassine njaan arinju, - Njaan athine cheyvaan aaye, - Nin thiru managunam athinaale, - Enikku nee manogunam cheyyaname.
Nirappu niranj oru antheeyum, - Punnyathvathinude raavum - Njangalude rakshaakaran mashiha, - Karthaave adiyaarkku nee tharika.
Velivil thaan prakaashichhu, - Velivil thanne paarkkunnu, - Velivinude suthar aayavarum, - Ninne thanne vannikkunnu.
Ninakku sthuthi nin anugrahangal, - Njangalude melum athaakaname, - Ihalokathilum athupole, - Paralokathilum athaakaname.
Ente Karthaave! ninakku sthuthi, - Ninakku sthuthi sthuthi ninakku sthuthi, - Aayirangalude aayiravum, - Alavu koodathe ninakku sthuthi.

h3:Psalms 91
Barekmor - Uyarappettavante Maravil irrikkunnavanum, / Daivathinte nizhalil mahathwa pedunnavanume! 

Barekmor - Njaan aashrayichu irikkunna daivam / ente sharanavum ente sanketha sthalavum ennu / karthhaavine kurichu nee parayuka. 

Enthennaal avan virudhhathhinte keniyil ninnum / vyartha samsaarathhil ninnum ninne veendukollum. 

Avan avante thoovalukal kondu ninne rekshikkum. / Avante chirakukalude keezhil nee maraykka pedum. / Avante sathyam ninte chuttum aayudham aayirikum. 

Nee raathriyile bhayathhil ninnum, / pakal parakkunna asthrathil ninnum / iruttil sancharikkunna vachanathhil ninnum / uchhayil oothunna kaattil ninnum bhayappeduka illa. 

Ninte oru bhaagathu aayirangalum, / ninte valathhu bhagathhu pathinaayirangalum veezhum. 

Ava ninkalekku aduka illa. / Ennalo ninte kannukal kondu nee kaanuka maathram cheyyum. / Dushtanmaarkkku ulla prathiphalathe nee kaanum. 

Enthennaal swantha vaasa sthalam / uyarangalil aakki irikunnu enna / ente sharanam aaya karthavu nee aakunnu. 

Dosham ninnodu adukka illa. / Shiksha ninte vasa sthalathhinu sameepikkayum illa. 

Enthennaal ninte sakala vazhikalilum / ninne kaakkendathinu / avan ninne kurichu avante malakhamaarodu kalpikum.

Ninte kaalil ninaku idarcha bhavikathe irippan / avar thangalude bhujangalin mel ninne vahikum.

Sarpathheyum analiyeyum nee chavittum. / Simhathheyum perumpampineyum nee methikum.

Avan enne anweshichhathu kondu / njaan avane rekshichhu belappeduthhum; / ente naamam arinjathukondu avan enne vilikkum.
