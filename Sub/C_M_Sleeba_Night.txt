h1:id="SN"~Sleeba - Night (12 AM) 

h2:(Kauma)
† Subaho...Pithavinum puthranum parishudhha roohaykkum sthuthi

People: Balaheenarum papikalum aaya njangalude mel anugrahangalum karunayum / randu lokangalilum ennum ennekkum choriyappedumaru aakaname – Amen​
    
Urakkam illatha unarvu ullavan aaya ente karthaave! ninte unarchaye sthothram cheyyuvan aayittu paapa muzhukalil ninnu njangalude urakkathe unarthename. Maranam illatha jeevan ullavane! ninte karunaye njangal vandhippaan aayittu, maranathinteyum, naashathinteyum urakkathil ninnu njangalude maranathe jeevippikkename. Pithavum puthranum parishudhha roohayume! swargathhilum bhoomiyilum sthuthikkappettavanum vaazhthappettavanum nee aakayal ninne sthuthikkunnavar aaya swargeeya maalaakamaarude mahathwam ulla vrindhangalodu orumichu vishudhhiyodu koodi ippozhum eppozhum ennekkum ninne sthuthichu vaazhthuvaan njangale yogyar aakkename.

People: Amen. ​
h3:Psalms 133 
Barekmor, Rathri kaalangalil karthaavinte bhavanathhil nilkkunnavar aayi karthaavinte sakala daasanmaarum aayullore! ningal karthaavine vaazhthuvin.

Barekmor, Shudha sthalathekku ningalude kaikal uyarthi karthaavine vazhthuvin.

Akaashavum bhumiyum srushhtichha karthaavu sehiyonil ninnu ninne anugrahikkum.
h3:Psalms 118, 116 
Karthaave! ente sthuthi ninte munpaake praveshikkukayum, ninte vachanathhaal enne jeevippikkukayum cheyyename. Ente apeksha ninte munpaake praveshikkukayum, ninte vachanathal enne rekshikkukayum cheyyename.

Ente naavu ninte vachanam uchharikkum. Enthennaal ninte kalpanakal ellaam neethiyodu koodiyava aakunnu.

Ninte kalpanakal enne nee patippikumpol ente adharangal ninte sthuthikal uchharikkum. Ninte kalpanakalil njaan ishta-ppedunn-athinaal ninte kai enne sahaayikkename.

Ente aathmavu ninte rakshakku aayittu kathirunnu. Ninte vedapramaanam njaan dhhyanichu. Ente aathmavu jeevichu ninne sthuthikkukayum ninte nyaaya vidhhi enne sahaayikkukayum cheyyename.

Kaanaathe poya aadupole njaan thettippoyi. Ninte bhruthyane anveshikkename. Enthennaal ninte kalpanakal onnum njaan marannilla.

Sakala jaathikalume! karthaavine sthuthippin. Sakala janangalume! avane ningal sthuthippin. Enthennaal avante krupa nammude mel balappettu irikkunnu. Avan sathyam aayittu ennekkum karthaavu aakunnu.

Daivame sthuthi ninakku yogyam aakunnu. Barekmor​. †Shubaho... Men olam... ​
