h1:id="Q1"~Hymn before Public Celebration of the Qurbana
(Answer Lord, and hear my plea, Halleluiah)
May our incense favour Thee - As was Aaron's sweet;
And our office like that - plea of the Nin-ne-vites
&emsp;As thou didst answer Jona,
&emsp;Answer us who call on Thee

Let My Cry Come Unto Thee Haleluiah
May the incense sent by us - To Thy Name so pure,
Sweet become, appeasing Thee - Graciousness evoke
&emsp;Favour us, O Lord, in love -
&emsp;God of all compassion Thou.

Glory to Thy gracious love, - Jesus, Lord and God;
Plenteous are Thy gifts indeed - Giv’n to all the world.
&emsp;Thou redeemedst those from wrath
&emsp;Ninevites who called on Thee

May our incense, favour Thee - As was Aaron’s sweet,
As was Zachariah's set - In the sacred shrine.
&emsp;Like the plea of Phinehas
&emsp;Staying from the people death

From the flaming glory bright - Flew the angel swift
Unto Mary, Naz’reth’s maid - Saying unto her,
&emsp;With thee is the Lord from Thee
&emsp;Comes the Saviour of the world.

As the fire enflamed the bush - Yet did not it burn,
So did God come down and well - In the virgin’s womb,
&emsp;He incarnate was of her
&emsp;Losing not the virgin’s seal.

Martyrs shunned this passing world, - All possessions spurned,
Left their parents, brethren too, - Left their kith and kin.
&emsp;Loved they, death for Jesus sake
&emsp;Solemnly their feasts are held

Martyrs, seeing Jesus hang - On the cross for them,
From his lan-ced side there flowed - Blood and water both
&emsp;Heartened, they said each to each,
&emsp;“Come, we’ll die now for the Lord”.

By Thy light we see the light, Jesus, full of light; ​
Thou, True Light, dost give the light ​- To Thy creatures all.​
&emsp;Lighten us with Thy gay light, ​
&emsp;Thou, the Fatherís light divine.

Thou who dwellest in the light Mansions holy, pure; ​
Keep us from all hateful thoughts,​ - From all passions vile,​
&emsp;Grant us cleanness in our hearts ​
&emsp;Deeds of righteousness to do.​

God, who didst receive the lamb Blameless Abel brought,​
Who the gift of Noah took,​ - Abramís sacrifice;​
&emsp;See our fast and hear our prayír,​
&emsp;Answer by Thy grace our pleas.

Come, ye sinners, and implore, Seek forgiveness here; ​
To one knocking at the door​ - Openeth the Lord.​
&emsp;He that asketh doth receive,​
&emsp;He that seeketh, he shall find.

Lord, grant good remembrance to All the faithful dead;​
Thy holy body they took​ - And Thy living blood.​
&emsp;May they stand on Thy right side​
&emsp;On that day Thy grace shall dawn.​

h3:HO EDONO​
Come, the time of prayer is here, ​
Come for pardon, have no fear; ​
Tis the time to ask anew, ​
Tis the time for mercy too

See the holy priest ascend, ​
Mounting stairs which heavenward tend, 
There the pure Qurban to raise ​
For whoever communicates.

Mercy here is full and free, ​
Come, beloved, come and see, ​
Give the kiss of peace divine, ​
Hearts sincere in love combine.

Let us now be reconciled ​
To each heavenly Father's child, ​
So, before God's throne of grace ​
Find compassion on His face.​

Lord, have mercy on us now, ​
Grant forgiveness as we bow, ​
Answer, Lord, our earnest plea; ​
Good art Thou-though frail we be.

Stand we all and sing we well, ​
Cry as one His grace to tell.​
Answer, Lord, our earnest plea; 
Good art Thou-though frail we be
