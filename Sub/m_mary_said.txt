h3:Mavurba (Luke. 1:46-55) 
Mariyam paranjathu enthennal, ente dehi karthavine pukazthunnu. Ente athmavu enne jeevipikkunnavan aaya daivathil santhoshichu. Enthennal avan  thante daasiyude thazchaye nokikandu irikkunnu. Kandalum ithu muthal sakala vamshangalum eniku bagyam tharum.

Enthennaal shakthimaanum thante naamam parishudhhavum aayirikkunnavan valiya kaaryangal enkal pravarthichu irikkunnu. Avante karuna avane bhayappedunnavarude mel thalamurakal aayum vamhshangal aayum undakunnu.

Avan thante bhujam kondu jayam undakki; thangalude hrudhaya vicharathil ahankaarikal aayavare chinnichu; avan balavaanmaare simhaasanangalil ninnu marichu idukayum, thaazhma ullavare uyarthukayum cheythu.

Avan vishappu ullavare nanmakal kondu sampoornar aakkukayum sampannanmare vyarthham aayi ayakkukayum cheythu, Avan thante dhaasanaya israeline sahaayikkukayum nammude pithaakkanmar aaya abrahaminodum avante santhathiyodum samsaricha prakaaram ennekkum thante karunaye orkkukayum cheythu.
